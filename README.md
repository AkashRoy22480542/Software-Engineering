🚀 Project Overview

The traditional agricultural supply chain in Bangladesh is heavily controlled by intermediaries, causing farmers to receive unfair prices while consumers pay significantly higher costs. This project introduces a technology-driven solution that bridges the gap between rural producers and urban consumers through a web-based marketplace platform.

The system enables farmers to directly list and sell their agricultural products while consumers can browse, order, review, and track products in real time. The platform also promotes transparency, organic farming awareness, and sustainable business practices.
